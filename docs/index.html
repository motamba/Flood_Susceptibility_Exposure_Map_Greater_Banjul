    About

    This map shows where flooding is most likely in Greater Banjul (Banjul, Kanifing Municipal Council and Kombo North), based on terrain and land cover. It also shows which assets lie in the most susceptible areas.

    Method (summary)

    A weighted overlay of terrain factors: height above nearest drainage, elevation, depression depth, wetness index and land cover. The index is classified into low, medium and high, and the weights are tested in a sensitivity analysis.

    Limitations

    • The elevation data has 30 m resolution, which limits detail in flat coastal areas.
    • No ground-truth flood data; plausibility checks only.
    • Weights are subjective and are tested for sensitivity.
